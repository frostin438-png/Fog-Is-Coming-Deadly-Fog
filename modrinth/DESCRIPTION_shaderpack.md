# Fog Is Coming Shaders

A moody, semi-realistic shader pack for **Oculus / Iris**, made as a companion to the
[Fog Is Coming: Deadly Fog](https://modrinth.com/mod/fog-is-coming) mod. It also works fine without the mod, as an ordinary shader pack without the fog.

## Features
- Soft shadows from the sun and moon, with a distorted shadow map so there is more detail near you.
- Grass, flowers, tall plants and leaves sway in the wind, and their shadows sway too.
- Water with animated waves, sky reflections and a sun glint.
- A procedural sky with sunset glow, and stars.
- Linear HDR lighting with filmic (ACES) tone mapping, warm block light and glow around bright lights.
- **With the Fog Is Coming mod:**
  - the mod's fog is drawn as a volumetric fog with drifting clumps;
  - the sun, moon and stars vanish as the fog thickens;
  - direct sunlight fades and the scene is lit by grey, diffuse fog;
  - light from torches and fires glows in the fog around them;
  - a cold film look with grain kicks in.

## Settings
Shadows, shadow softness, resolution and distance, sun path angle, waving plants, water waves, volumetric fog and its quality, light glow, film look, brightness. Includes English and Russian translations.

## Requirements
- Minecraft 1.20.1 with **Oculus** (tested with 1.8.0) and **Embeddium**. It should also work on Iris for Fabric, but that has not been tested.
- Not made for OptiFine.
